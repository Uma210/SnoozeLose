Your morning mission

Choose your wake-up time and save an alarm. When an alert arrives, open SnoozeLose and finish one assigned game. Snoozing delays the reminder but never adds games.

iOS always provides a system Stop button. On older iOS versions, standard notification delivery is less persistent than an AlarmKit alarm.
